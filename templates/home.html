Transparent Workflow

How LifeDrop Coordinates Emergencies

Four simple steps from critical request to hospital transfusion.

1
Post Request

Requesters provide verified patient, blood group, hospital details, and critical deadline.

2
Instant Match

Our engine identifies eligible, verified donors in the hospital's vicinity with exact group match.

3
Secure Connect

Donors confirm willingness in real-time, sharing contact coordinates safely without public exposure.

4
Clinical Transfusion

Donation is completed at the hospital/blood bank following mandatory laboratory cross-matching.

Check Your Eligibility

Can You Donate Blood Today?

Before registering or dispatching for donation, ensure you meet the clinical criteria established by national transfusion safety guidelines:

Age: Between 18 and 65 years old.
Weight: Minimum 45 kg (50 kg for platelet donation).
Cooldown: At least 90 days (3 months) since your last whole blood donation.
Health Status: Good general health, normal blood pressure, and hemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL.
View Complete Medical Deferral Guide →
Clinical Safety First

LifeDrop facilitates volunteer communication. We do not replace clinical cross-matching. Even if a donor has a compatible self-reported blood group, hospitals must perform certified laboratory pre-transfusion testing to guarantee patient safety.

Did you know?
One single blood donation of 350-450 mL can be separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets, potentially saving up to three distinct lives.
