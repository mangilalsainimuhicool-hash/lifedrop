Data Governance

Privacy Policy & Health Information Handling

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Core Commitment: Zero Public Scraping of Donor Contacts

At LifeDrop, we strictly safeguard personal identifying information. Unauthenticated public visitors or automated web spiders can never view donor telephone numbers or detailed residential addresses. Only authenticated users with legitimate, verified emergency coordination roles may request contact with donors.

2. Information We Collect
  • Account Data: Name, verified email address, emergency contact phone number, and account role.
  • Donor Medical Profiles: Blood group, date of birth (for age compliance), city/district, self-reported last donation date, and availability status.
  • Emergency Request Data: Patient name, hospital name, attending physician case notes, units needed, and coordinator contact details.
  • Audit & Telemetry Logs: Timestamped action events, authorized actor IDs, and IP addresses to maintain security forensics and combat misuse.
3. Explicit Consent & Control

Donors maintain absolute control over their availability. You can toggle your emergency status to "Temporarily Paused" at any time from your dashboard, which immediately hides your profile from emergency search results.

4. No Commercial Sharing or Sale of Data

LifeDrop is a humanitarian coordination network. We never sell, lease, or monetize personal health data or contact lists to pharmaceutical marketers, data brokers, or third-party advertisers.
