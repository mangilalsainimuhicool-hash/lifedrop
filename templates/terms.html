Terms of Service & Clinical Disclaimer

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Nature of the Service

LifeDrop is an online voluntary emergency notification and coordination service. LifeDrop is NOT a healthcare provider, hospital, or blood bank. We do not store, transport, screen, cross-match, or transfuse blood.

2. Prohibition on Commercial Blood Trade

Users agree that all blood donation coordination facilitated through LifeDrop is entirely voluntary and unpaid. Soliciting, demanding, or accepting financial compensation or material goods in exchange for blood or blood components is strictly prohibited under law and results in immediate permanent ban and referral to medical legal authorities.

3. Mandatory Clinical Cross-Matching

Hospitals, attending medical officers, and requesters bear the sole responsibility for executing complete pre-transfusion laboratory testing, blood grouping confirmation, screening for infectious diseases (such as HBV, HCV, HIV, Syphilis, and Malaria), and antibody compatibility testing before administering any blood product.

4. Limitation of Liability

While LifeDrop utilizes best-effort algorithms and automated notifications to alert matching donors quickly, we cannot guarantee donor response times, physical donor arrival, or hospital transfusion outcomes. In critical situations, always contact regional emergency services and hospital blood bank supervisors concurrently.
