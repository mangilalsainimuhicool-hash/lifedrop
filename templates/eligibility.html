Clinical Safety First

Blood Donor Eligibility Guidelines

Protecting both blood donors and recipients is the foremost priority in transfusion medicine. Review standard clinical criteria before pledging your donation.

Who Can Donate?

Age Requirement

Must be between 18 and 65 years old (regular donors may donate up to 70 with physician consent).

Minimum Weight

Must weigh at least 45 kg (approx. 100 lbs) for safe 350 mL whole blood collection; 50 kg for 450 mL.

Donation Intervals (Cooldown)

At least 90 days (3 months) between whole blood donations for males, and 120 days (4 months) for females.

Hemoglobin Levels

Hemoglobin must test at or above 12.5 g/dL before collection to prevent post-donation fatigue or anemia.

Temporary Deferrals
  • Recent Cold / Fever / Flu: Wait at least 7 days after complete recovery and cessation of antibiotics.
  • Dental Extraction / Surgery: Defer for 3 to 7 days depending on procedure; wait 6 months for major surgery.
  • Tattoo / Body Piercing: Wait 6 to 12 months after receiving a tattoo or piercing.
  • Pregnancy / Lactation: Defer during pregnancy and for 12 months following childbirth.
  • Vaccinations: Deferral depends on vaccine type (wait 14 days after non-live vaccines; 4 weeks for live attenuated).
Permanent Deferrals
  • Individuals diagnosed with or testing positive for Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, or HIV.
  • Chronic cardiac diseases, severe chronic kidney disease, or bleeding disorders (e.g. Hemophilia).
  • History of injectable illicit drug use.
  • Active or past malignancies (cancers), unless cleared by a specialist oncologist.
Mandatory Clinical Protocol Notice

Self-evaluation through LifeDrop does not constitute medical clearance. Licensed phlebotomists and medical officers at certified blood banks or hospital transfusion services will conduct a pre-donation medical history screening, pulse check, blood pressure check, and hemoglobin test prior to any collection.
